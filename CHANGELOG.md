# NetherBags changelog

## 1.0.0 - First release

- The bags, bank and guild bank in two modes, One bag and Insight, switched at the top right.
- Insight: places, category row, search, sortable columns, groups that open and close, quick
  filters, Sell all junk at vendors.
- One bag: every slot in one grid, special bags in their own sections, bank tabs as sections.
- Your own groups with rules; item marks (lock, junk, always put in a group).
- Junk sold at vendors by itself; Sort; Stash trade goods at the bank.
- Every character's bags, bank, mailbox and money saved: offline views, Everywhere search,
  "owned by" counts on tooltips; guild banks once seen.
- Profiles for the whole account, with Export and Import.
- NetherBags' own look or NetherUI's theme (asked once at install when NetherUI is there).
- NetherSuite tile and settings search; plugin interface.
- Can't-use-yet markers (red, with the game's reason), quest-needed items protected, outgrown gear and "junk once" in Smarter junk (setting).
- Context fade at vendors, the mailbox, trades and the auction house; rows that stay put while the window is open.
- Free a slot (delete the cheapest junk when the bags are full, after a click), tracked items in the footer with a low warning.
- Move what you see to the bank, mailbox or trade (and a group from its header), Send all to an alt, mail-to-alt counting.
- Search: or, brackets, tooltip text, required level, saved as a group; a Looted this session group.
- Bank tab sockets with their own slots, "would free N" sort hint, merge after mail or trade (setting).
- Everywhere filters: tick the kinds of items and the places (bags, bank, guild, mailbox) to look in.
- Bag menu (the bag icon by the title): the kinds of items each bag holds, and a colour for its slots in One bag.
- Auto open and close with the crafting window, the character sheet and the map; other bag addons noticed with a choice of which to keep.
